## 2. What India is talking about right now

**MS Dhoni retires from the IPL, becomes CSK mentor (17 Nov).** CSK's post "Captain Dhoni: Legacy. Mentor Dhoni: Future." is everywhere, and CSK is reported to be retiring the No. 7 jersey. It's the biggest emotional moment on Indian social this week.
- Shelf life: 48 to 72 hours.
- Our angle: a quiet Story in the same two-line format, product only: "Moksha 100 ml: Legacy. Moksha 8 ml: Future."
- Rules: no name, no photos of him, no nicknames, no "tribute" that looks like an endorsement. Post by Thursday or skip it.

**The "Shanaya" trend (Instagram, peaking now).** "Shanaya" has become the internet's name for the polished, judgy person who looks down on anyone "from a small town". The reels: someone recounts what a Shanaya said to them, then claps back. Shanaya Kapoor joined it on 17 Nov, which pushed it further.
- Shelf life: one to two weeks.
- Our angle: an Indian-made brand with "Paris" in the name is the perfect clapback. That's skit 1.

**Wedding season opens Friday 20 Nov.** First muhurat cluster roughly 21 to 26 Nov, a second in the first half of December, then a pause from mid-December to mid-January (Kharmas).
- Our angle: guest-side shaadi humour (gifts, getting ready, the compliment, the aunty question). Don't tie perfume to the ritual itself; just say "wedding season is here".

**Delhi air: "very poor" to "severe".** AQI 351 on 15 Nov, 419 with 33 stations "severe" on 13 Nov, GRAP-3 rules on.
- Our angle: one dry joke, no health claims. "Spraying perfume and then wearing an N95: the most Delhi form of self-love."

**IPL trade season.** Hardik to Punjab Kings, Shami and Siddharth to Rajasthan Royals (RR announced it with a "trade" pun). Retention deadline was 17 Nov; the auction is in December.
- Our angle: the "trade announcement" card. "Official trade: your 5-year-old deo to Moksha. Fee: undisclosed." Works as a Story or post until the auction.

**Finnish embassy "planning day" (9 to 11 Nov).** Over 11.7M views on the original post. We already made ours. This one's done, so don't post more.

## 5. What sells perfume on Reels right now

From fragrance-category research (Spate, Scento):

- **The compliment hook.** "The perfume that made a stranger stop me." Only ever real. Our version: film genuine reactions in the store or on the street with permission, or the shaadi compliment skit.
- **The reaction hook.** People stopping someone to ask what they're wearing. Same rule: real people, real reactions, their consent.
- **"Smells like..."** Scent explained through a memory, not a note list. Our version: Delhi memories, written from each scent's real notes. Example shape: "smells like the first morning you take the shawl out." Check it against the product notes first.
- **Persona tags.** "Smells like old money." Our Indian version: "smells like a South Delhi farmhouse wedding" vs "smells like you won the bargaining in Lajpat Nagar".
- **Lists people save.** "A scent for every shaadi function": haldi, mehendi, sangeet, baraat, reception. Needs our real product picks per function.
- **Layering routines are growing.** Shower gel, then body lotion or oil, then perfume. One layering tutorial in the research hit 2M views. We own this: the Moksha body wash, moisturiser and perfume, and the Signature Ritual gift set.
- **Blind sniff tests and blind buys.** Friends blindfolded, rating scents. Real reactions only.
- **Gourmand scents are the fastest-growing category** (pistachio searches up 852.5% year on year, plus vanilla, honey, caramel), skin scents and soft oud blends are rising, fresh citrus is being used as a base layer. Worth knowing for stock and for which bottles we push. We're not making any note claims until we've checked the product sheets.
- **Skip completely:** "dupe" content. It's the biggest perfume format online and it breaks our rule: we never compare ourselves to designer bottles or name them.

## 6. The skits, fully written

All shot vertical on the back camera. Each shot: start recording 2 seconds before the line, keep rolling 2 seconds after. Two or three takes of every line.

### Skit 1: "Paris se hai?" (rides the Shanaya trend)
**Length:** 18 to 22 sec. **Cast:** Tannay + one friend playing the polished, slightly snobby one (guy or girl). **Locations:** any party-looking corner (fairy lights help), Lajpat Nagar street or metro exit, the store.

| # | Shot | Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Both in frame, chest up. Friend leans in and sniffs near Tannay's collar. On screen: "A Shanaya smelled my perfume" | Friend: "Wait. Kaunsa perfume hai?" |
| 2 | Tannay, relaxed | "Mi Amor Paris." |
| 3 | Friend, impressed, a bit posh | "Obviously imported. You can just tell. Paris se mangwaya?" |
| 4 | Tannay nods. Slowly. Holds the nod one beat too long. | (silence) |
| 5 | Friend | "Next time jao toh mere liye bhi le aana." |
| 6 | Tannay | "Kal chalein?" |
| 7 | Friend, shocked | "Kal?! Visa?" |
| 8 | Tannay, deadpan | "Visa nahi. Metro card." |
| 9 | Hard cut. Lajpat Nagar metro exit or street, then the store sign readable in frame. On screen: "Paris. Lajpat Nagar-II." | Street noise only |
| 10 | Inside. Friend holding three bottles, spraying a fourth on the wrist, not looking up. | "Achha. Paris hi hai." |

End card: "Mi Amor Paris. O-17, Lajpat Nagar-II."
Caption direction: "Paris, but the metro goes there 🤍 Who in your family asks 'imported hai?' first?"

**Solo version (8 sec, no friend needed):** On screen "A Shanaya asked which imported perfume I'm wearing". Tannay sprays Moksha, looks at the camera, tiny smile. Cut to the store sign: "Imported from Lajpat Nagar-II."

### Skit 2: 5 stages of buying perfume for someone else
**Sound:** "5 stages of being a" (Ramdam link: instagram.com/reels/audio/28492404660419811). **Cast:** 1 customer + staff (hands only is fine). **Location:** store. 2 to 3 sec per stage, stage name on screen.

1. **Confidence.** Walks in: "Paanch minute ka kaam hai. I know exactly what she likes."
2. **Research.** Holding 11 tester strips fanned out like a bouquet, sniffing each one very seriously.
3. **Nose has left the chat.** Sniffs the coffee beans like a wine expert. Sniffs own sleeve. Sniffs the beans again. Confused. (No coffee beans in store? Sleeve and wrist only.)
4. **Panic.** On the phone: "Usko floral pasand hai ya woody? ...Woh kya hota hai?"
5. **Acceptance.** Staff hands over the bag: "Wahi jo sabse pehle smell kiya tha?" Him: "...Haan."

End card: "Skip stages 2 to 4. Comment SCENT for the quiz."

## 7. Trends we could start ourselves

**1. "Imported hai?"** Every Indian relative's first question about anything nice. Our first video is skit 1. Then invite people to post the Indian-made things their relatives were sure were imported. It's funny, it's proud, and it's ours, because our name literally says Paris and our address says Lajpat Nagar. Can run as a recurring series.

**2. "Papa ka review."** Spray a scent on a dad's wrist and let him give his one-word verdict. Dads in this country are deadpan, which is the joke. Turn it into a carousel too, the dad translation guide:
- "Theek hai." = 10/10, he loves it.
- "Kitne ka hai?" = he wants one.
- "Mujhe toh kuch smell nahi aa raha." = spray more.
- "Apni mummy ko dikhana." = it's a gift for her now.
- "Hmm." = he'll wear it every day and deny it.
Ask followers to film their own dads and tag us; we repost the best ones (with permission).

**3. The tester strip bouquet.** Every customer leaves holding a fan of strips. Photograph the biggest bouquet of the day for Stories: "Today's record: 23 strips." A small store ritual people start doing on purpose.

## 9. Rules for every video

- Back camera, vertical, 1080p at 60 fps or 4K at 30. Wipe the lens. Tap and hold to lock exposure.
- Window light or store light. Keep the ring light off glossy bottles.
- Bottle labels facing the camera, held steady.
- Send me the original files, not WhatsApp-compressed ones (send as document).
- Anyone on camera says yes first. Customers especially.
- Never: celebrity names or faces, competitor or designer names, the word "dupe", made-up reactions or reviews, perfume tied to puja or ritual, health claims (pollution included), exact lasting hours we haven't tested.
- Moment posts go up while the moment is alive. A day late is fine, a week late is cringe.
